Surgeons, soldiers, and shoppers — we've built for all of them. Our work thrives where failure isn't an option and complexity is the starting point.

We specialize in systems that survive reality — whether that's a high-pressure clinical environment, an armored vehicle interior, or a luxury retail launch. Every pixel earns its place. Every interaction is tested against the worst case, not the demo.

Based in Bengaluru with partner agencies at project locations across Europe and India, rabbitsfoot brings a "don't-fail" discipline to every engagement. After years designing for hospitals, armored vehicles, and retail floors, we've forgotten how to make things that break.
