## Anti-Patterns Verdict

**Does this look AI-generated? Mostly no — it has a real point of view.** The cosmic identity, the spaceship cursor, the film grain, and (crucially) the About copy are distinctive and human. This is not a template. Two places pull it back toward slop: generic portfolio filler copy, and a hero stat block.

**Deterministic scan (5 findings):**
- **Overused font: Inter** (×2, warning) — false positive for this project. Inter is committed identity in DESIGN.md, paired with Questrial + mono. Identity-preservation wins.
- **Marketing buzzword: "game-changer"** (warning) — inside a client testimonial quote. Real, but it's a verbatim quote.
- **Numbered section markers 01/02/03** (advisory) — the blog-learnings cards. Borderline: blog posts aren't an ordered process, so the numbering reads as editorial scaffold.
- **Dark mode + colored glow** (warning) — false positive. It's the comet trail glow `rgba(200,220,255,0.8)`, intrinsic to the cosmic hero, not decorative AI glow.

**What the detector pointed me to that matters more:** the hero **stat block** (`8+ years / 25+ / 20+ / 5+`) is the hero-metric template your PRODUCT.md explicitly rejects ("no hero-metric blocks"). It's the single most SaaS-landing moment on the page.

## Overall Impression

A confident, characterful portfolio that mostly clears the "AI made this" bar. The biggest opportunity is **copy consistency**: the About section ("Surgeons, soldiers, and shoppers — I've built for all of them") and the booking CTA ("30 minutes. No agenda.") are genuinely excellent and specific, but the Projects intro and quote section fall back into interchangeable filler that any portfolio could run. The voice is the differentiator for design-peer readers; right now it flickers.

## What's Working

1. **The About voice.** "Systems that survive reality", "a don't-fail discipline... after designing for the defence, I've forgotten how to make things that break." Specific, opinionated, memorable. This is the POV the rest of the page should match.
2. **The booking CTA copy.** "30 minutes. No agenda. Just a conversation about what you're building." Low-pressure, specific, human. Exactly right for the audience.
3. **Committed cosmic identity.** Spaceship cursor, film grain, warm-dark palette, astronaut — a cohesive world, not a borrowed template.

## Priority Issues

- **[P1] Copy voice flickers between distinctive and generic.** The Projects intro ("where strategy meets creativity to deliver impactful digital experiences... user-first approach, ensuring both aesthetics and functionality shine") and the quote section ("blend creativity, strategy, and innovation to build products that not only function beautifully but also make a lasting impact") are interchangeable AI portfolio filler. They directly undercut the "hold a POV / show the thinking" principle for a skeptical design-peer audience.
  - **Fix:** Rewrite Projects intro and quote copy in the same specific, concrete voice as the About section. Name the work, not the adjectives.
  - **Suggested command:** /impeccable clarify

- **[P1] No `prefers-reduced-motion` anywhere.** Zero matches across index.html, site-globals.css, styles.css. The hero alone runs grain animation, 3 comets, CSS stars, a sparkles canvas, a fireflies canvas, plus an astronaut — with no reduced-motion fallback. This is both an accessibility gap and a performance load on mobile.
  - **Fix:** Add a `@media (prefers-reduced-motion: reduce)` block that stills the comets/sparkles/fireflies/grain and keeps a static cosmic backdrop.
  - **Suggested command:** /impeccable audit

- **[P2] Hero stat block is the hero-metric template you rejected.** `8+ / 25+ / 20+ / 5+` big-number + small-label is the exact SaaS-landing cliché PRODUCT.md bans. For design peers it reads as resume-padding, not proof.
  - **Fix:** Either cut it, or convert the numbers into something specific (named domains, named clients, a one-line proof) rather than a 4-up metric grid.
  - **Suggested command:** /impeccable layout

- **[P2] Hero may be over-layered.** ~10 stacked decorative layers (grainCanvas, fireflies, nebula, horizon, stars, 3 comets, scanlines, sparkles, gradient, astronaut, glow) risk burying the one thing that matters — "I CREATE EXPERIENCES" and the path into the work. Richness is on-brand; competition for attention is not.
  - **Fix:** Audit which layers earn their place; consider dialing back 2-3 so the headline and CTA lead.
  - **Suggested command:** /impeccable distill

- **[P3] "I CREATE EXPERIENCES" is a slightly generic headline.** Every designer "creates experiences." For a design-peer audience this is the one place to be sharper, though the typeword animation rescues some specificity.
  - **Suggested command:** /impeccable clarify

## Persona Red Flags

**Jordan (First-Timer / recruiter skim):** The hero is beautiful but the value prop ("I CREATE EXPERIENCES") is abstract; the concrete proof (domains, clients) is lower down. A 5-second skim gets atmosphere, not "what is this person senior in."

**Sam (Accessibility / reduced-motion):** No `prefers-reduced-motion` — the page animates continuously with no opt-out. The spaceship cursor replaces the system pointer site-wide, which can hurt target visibility. Decorative layers are correctly `aria-hidden`, which is good.

**Casey (Distracted mobile):** Multiple always-on canvases (grain, fireflies, sparkles, orbital globe) are a real battery/perf cost on a phone; the continuous animation never settles.

## Minor Observations

- Blog-card 01/02/03 numbering — swap for dates or categories; posts aren't a sequence.
- About photo alt text is just "Aayush R" — fine, could be richer.
