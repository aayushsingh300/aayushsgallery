# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

Design leaders and design peers — design directors, senior/staff product designers, and hiring panels evaluating Aayush R for senior product design roles. They arrive skeptical and time-pressed, fluent in craft, and quick to spot a template. Their context is a fast first scan ("is this person serious?") followed by a deeper read of one or two case studies if the surface earns it. The job to be done: judge whether the thinking, rigor, and execution behind the work are real.

## Product Purpose

A personal portfolio for Aayush R, a senior product designer (8+ years), built as a multi-page static site (custom Node `server.js`). It exists to prove design ability through the medium itself: a cosmic/space-themed home that sets a confident point of view, leading into in-depth, interactive app case studies (Parkbeheer, Chargeur, Prahari, Rojnica, Penknife, Vigil, Muspell, TitanEye). Success is a design leader finishing a case study convinced the work is rigorous and the person is worth a conversation.

## Brand Personality

Bold, cosmic, ambitious. Cinematic confidence without arrogance — the site should feel like it was made by someone who can hold a strong aesthetic point of view and still ship precise, working interfaces. Voice is direct and specific, not salesy. The space theme is identity, not decoration: it signals ambition and scale, but the work stays the hero.

## Anti-references

- **Generic template portfolio.** The interchangeable Webflow/Framer look — same hero, same uniform card grid, no point of view. If a visitor could mistake this for a template, it has failed.
- **Corporate SaaS landing.** Navy-and-blue gradients, hero-metric blocks, buzzword copy ("seamless", "enterprise-grade"), feature-card monotony. This is a designer's portfolio, not a B2B pitch deck.

## Design Principles

- **The medium is the proof.** The portfolio itself must demonstrate the craft it claims; sloppy execution anywhere undercuts every case study.
- **The work is the hero.** The cosmic theme frames and elevates, but never competes with or buries the actual project work.
- **Earn the deep read.** The home and case-study openers must convert a fast skeptical scan into a committed read; lead with substance, not throat-clearing.
- **Hold a point of view.** Make committed aesthetic choices over safe defaults; a memorable opinion beats inoffensive polish for this audience.
- **Show the thinking, not just the screens.** For design peers, the reasoning behind decisions is the differentiator — make it visible.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Not a current priority per the project owner; craft and impact come first. Note for later: the home leans heavily on motion (nebula, comets, animated stars, spaceship cursor), so a `prefers-reduced-motion` pass and a contrast check on body copy over the dark cosmic background are the highest-value accessibility follow-ups when this is revisited.
