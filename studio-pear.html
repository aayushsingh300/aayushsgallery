How the work moves

Close to the problem.
Closer to the people.

01Observe

Go where the work is.

Watch the hand-offs, workarounds and pressure points. The real environment becomes the brief.

02Make

Give the idea weight.

Prototype early enough to expose the wrong assumptions while changing them is still cheap.

03Stress

Test the ugly moments.

Gloves on. Alarm sounding. Network gone. Design for the moment the polished demo never shows.

04Ship

Stay until it is real.

Work beside engineering, protect the intent and tune the product through the final mile.
