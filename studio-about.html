About the Studio

We're rabbitsfoot.
We don't do decorative.

A design studio founded on the principle that great work happens when failure isn't an option. We build products for the hardest domains, defense, healthcare, and enterprise among them, where every decision has real consequences.

Our Story

Born from complexity,
built for clarity.

rabbitsfoot started in 2017 with a simple observation: the products that matter most, the ones surgeons depend on, soldiers rely on, and enterprises run on, were getting the worst design. Complex domains were treated as an excuse for complexity in the interface.

We decided to change that. We built a studio that specializes in going deep — understanding the operational reality of a factory floor, a cockpit, a hospital ward — and designing systems that make those environments feel effortless.

Today, from our base in Bengaluru, we work with healthcare providers, enterprise teams, defense programs, and consumer brands, partnering with agencies at each project's location. Our work spans clinical platforms serving thousands of patients, retail experiences for India's largest eyewear brand, and defense engagements published under NDA with details altered.

What We Believe

01

Precision over decoration

Every pixel earns its place. We don't add visual weight for the sake of it. Our interfaces are clear because they're designed to be used, not admired from a distance.

02

Systems that survive reality

We test against the worst case, not the happy path. Our designs hold up in low-light cockpits, with gloved hands, under time pressure, and across 200+ user roles.

03

Go where the users are

Factory floors. Hospital wards. Military bases. Agricultural fields. We do our research on-site, not from a conference room with a whiteboard and assumptions.
